About

Martin Curkovic is a Melbourne-based house music producer crafting deep, groove-driven sounds built for the dance floor. Since launching his solo project in late 2023 with the debut single Chemicals, he has been steadily gaining momentum, dropping a string of high-energy releases that have made waves in the local club scene.

Blending rhythmic basslines, smooth vocals, and syncopated percussion, Martin’s dance floor weapons channel the raw energy of underground house and garage while pushing forward with his signature sound. With growing support from DJs across the scene, his tracks continue to resonate with dance music lovers and clubgoers alike.

With an ever-expanding catalog and an unstoppable passion for the craft, Martin Curkovic is just getting started - expect more grooves and anthems for the afters in 2026.
